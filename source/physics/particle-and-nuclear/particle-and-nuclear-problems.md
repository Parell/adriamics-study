What is the total energy of a particle at rest?

:::solution
Set $p=0$ in $E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4$. The positive-energy solution is $E=mc^2$.
:::

-->

Can a reaction with total initial charge $+1e$ produce final particles with total charge $0$?

:::solution
No. Electric charge is conserved, so the final total must also be $+1e$.
:::

-->

What fraction of a radioactive sample remains after three half-lives?

:::solution
Each half-life halves the amount, so the remaining fraction is $(1/2)^3=1/8$.
:::

<!--

title: "Compute Nuclear Binding Energy"
exam: exam-i
skills: [Nuclear physics, Binding energy]
-->

A nucleus has constituent mass total $10.000\ \text{u}$ and measured nuclear mass $9.950\ \text{u}$. Estimate its binding energy using $1\ \text{u}\,c^2=931.5\ \text{MeV}$.

:::solution
The mass defect is $\Delta m=0.050\ \text{u}$, so

$$
B=\Delta m c^2=(0.050)(931.5)\ \text{MeV}\approx46.6\ \text{MeV}.
$$
:::

<!--

title: "Check Beta-minus Bookkeeping"
exam: exam-i
skills: [Radioactive decay, Conservation laws]
-->

Complete the beta-minus decay of a neutron: $n\rightarrow p+\ ?$.

:::solution
The products are $p+e^-+\bar\nu_e$. Charge is conserved because the electron contributes $-1$ while the proton contributes $+1$, and lepton number is conserved by the electron antineutrino.
:::
