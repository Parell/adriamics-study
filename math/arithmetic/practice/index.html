    1.1Write 8,405.72 in Expanded Form

    Exam I | Problem 1.1 |
    • Place Value
    • Expanded Form

    Write $8{,}405.72$ in expanded form.

    - -

    Solution

    Break the number into the value of each digit:

    $$ 8{,}405.72 = 8000 + 400 + 5 + 0.7 + 0.02 $$

    This matches the thousands, hundreds, ones, tenths, and hundredths places.

    • Integers

    $$ -3\ \_\ -8 $$

    $$ -3 > -8 $$

    - -

    $$ |-14| = 14 $$

    1.4Add Whole Numbers

    Exam I | Problem 1.4 |
    • Addition
    • Place Value

    Compute:

    $$ 467 + 289 $$
    - -

    Solution

    Add by place value:

    $$ 7 + 9 = 16 $$

    Write $6$ and carry $1$.

    $$ 6 + 8 + 1 = 15 $$

    Write $5$ and carry $1$.

    $$ 4 + 2 + 1 = 7 $$

    So,

    $$ 467 + 289 = 756 $$

    1.6Subtract Whole Numbers

    Exam I | Problem 1.6 |
    • Subtraction
    • Place Value

    Compute:

    $$ 700 - 458 $$
    - -

    Solution

    Regroup because $0 < 8$.

    $$ 700 = 6\text{ hundreds} + 9\text{ tens} + 10\text{ ones} $$

    Now subtract by place value:

    $$ 10 - 8 = 2 $$
    $$ 9 - 5 = 4 $$
    $$ 6 - 4 = 2 $$

    So,

    $$ 700 - 458 = 242 $$

    1.10Decide Whether 29 Is Prime or Composite

    Exam I | Problem 1.10 |
    • Prime and Composite Numbers

    Is $29$ prime or composite?

    - -

    Solution

    Check whether $29$ has any factors besides $1$ and itself. It is not divisible by $2$, $3$, or $5$, so its only positive factors are $1$ and $29$.

    Therefore, $29$ is prime.

    - -

    2.7Estimate and Then Multiply

    Exam II | Problem 2.7 |
    • Estimation
    • Multiplication

    Estimate $49 \times 21$ using compatible numbers, then find the exact product.

    - -

    Solution

    Round to nearby easy numbers:

    $$ 49 \approx 50,\quad 21 \approx 20 $$

    Estimate:

    $$ 50 \times 20 = 1000 $$

    Now compute exactly:

    $$ 49 \times 21 = 49(20+1)=980+49=1029 $$

    The estimate is close to the exact value.

    3.2Compute Simple Interest

    Final | Problem 3.2 |
    • Percents
    • Simple Interest

    Find the simple interest on $\$450$ at $6\%$ for $2$ years.

    - -

    Solution

    Use the formula $I=Prt$:

    $$ I = 450(0.06)(2) $$
    $$ I = 54 $$

    The interest is $\$54$. The total amount is

    $$ 450 + 54 = 504 $$

    So the total is $\$504$.

    - -

    • Problem-Solving Workflow

    A school buys $95$ pencils in packs of $8$.

    How many full packs can it make, and how many pencils remain?

    - -

    • Order of Operations
    • Exponents
    • Roots

    Compute:

    $$ 2^3 + 3(5-2)^2 - \sqrt{81} $$
    - -

    Solution

    Follow the order of operations.

    First evaluate the parentheses:

    $$ 5-2=3 $$

    Then the exponent:

    $$ 3^2=9 $$

    Now multiply:

    $$ 3(9)=27 $$

    $$ \sqrt{81}=9 $$

    Finish by adding and subtracting:

    4.2Apply Two Percent Changes in a Row

    Final | Problem 4.2 |
    • Percents
    • Problem-Solving Workflow

    A $\$50$ item is increased by $20\%$ and then decreased by $10\%$.

    What is the final price?

    - -

    Solution

    First find the $20\%$ increase:

    $$ 0.20(50)=10 $$

    So the new price is

    $$ 50+10=60 $$

    Now find the $10\%$ decrease of $60$:

    $$ 0.10(60)=6 $$

    Subtract:

    $$ 60-6=54 $$

    The final price is $\$54$.

    4.4Estimate and Compute a Scientific-Notation Product

    Final | Problem 4.4 |
    • Scientific Notation
    • Estimation
    • Exponents

    Estimate and then compute:

    $$ (4.5 \times 10^3)(2 \times 10^2) $$
    - -

    Solution

    Estimate by rounding $4.5$ to $5$:

    $$ (5 \times 10^3)(2 \times 10^2) = 10 \times 10^5 = 10^6 $$

    Now compute exactly:

    $$ (4.5 \times 2)\times 10^{3+2} = 9 \times 10^5 $$

    So the exact product is

    $$ 9 \times 10^5 = 900{,}000 $$
