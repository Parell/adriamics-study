    1.1Combine Like Terms in a Polynomial

    Exam I | Problem 1.1 |
    • Like Terms

    Simplify the expression and report the coefficient of $x$:

    $$ 4x - 2x + 9x + 6 $$
    - -

    Solution

    Combine the like terms:

    $$ 4x - 2x + 9x = 11x $$

    So the simplified expression is

    $$ 11x + 6 $$

    The coefficient of $x$ is $11$.

    1.7Combine Fractional Coefficients

    Exam I | Problem 1.7 |
    • Like Terms
    • Fractions

    Simplify the expression and give the coefficient of $x$:

    $$ \frac{2}{3}x + \frac{1}{3}x + 4 $$
    - -

    Solution

    The $x$-terms have a common denominator:

    $$ \frac{2}{3}x + \frac{1}{3}x = \left(\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right)x = 1x $$

    So the coefficient of $x$ is $1$.

    2.2Fraction Equation with Two Terms

    Exam II | Problem 2.2 |

    • Linear Equations
    • Inverse Operations

    Solve for $x$:

    $$ \frac{x - 1}{4} + \frac{x + 3}{2} = 8 $$
    - -

    Solution

    Multiply the entire equation by $4$ to clear the denominators:

    $$ x - 1 + 2(x + 3) = 32 $$

    $$ x - 1 + 2x + 6 = 32 $$

    $$ 3x + 5 = 32 $$

    2.3Solve by Substitution in a System

    Exam II | Problem 2.3 |
    • Systems of equations
    • Substitution

    In the system

    $$ \begin{aligned} y &= x + 2 \\ x + y &= 14 \end{aligned} $$

    what is the value of $x$?

    - -

    Solution

    Substitute $y = x + 2$ into the second equation:

    $$ x + (x + 2) = 14 $$

    Combine like terms:

    $$ 2x + 2 = 14 $$

    Subtract $2$:

    $$ 2x = 12 $$

    Divide by $2$:

    $$ x = 6 $$

    2.4Factor a Quadratic and Select the Smaller Root

    Exam II | Problem 2.4 |
    • Common factoring methods
    • Zero-product property

    The equation

    $$ x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0 $$

    has two solutions. What is the smaller solution?

    - -

    Solution

    Factor the quadratic:

    $$ x^2 - 7x + 12 = (x - 3)(x - 4) $$

    Use the zero-product property:

    $$ x - 3 = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad x - 4 = 0 $$

    So the solutions are $x = 3$ and $x = 4$.

    The smaller solution is $3$.

    Exam II | Problem 2.6 |

    Let

    $$ f(x) = 2x + 1 $$

    and

    $$ g(x) = 3x - 4. $$

    What is the value of $f(g(2))$?

    - -

    Solution

    Now substitute that result into $f$:

    $$ f(2) = 2(2) + 1 = 5 $$

    2.7Simplify and Evaluate a Rational Expression

    Exam II | Problem 2.7 |
    • Fractions
    • Distributing and factoring

    For $x \ne 3$, evaluate

    $$ \frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3} $$

    at $x = 5$.

    - -

    Solution

    Factor the numerator:

    $$ x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3) $$

    So, for $x \ne 3$,

    $$ \frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3} = x + 3 $$

    Now substitute $x = 5$:

    $$ 5 + 3 = 8 $$

    3.2Rectangle Dimensions from a Perimeter Condition

    Final | Problem 3.2 |
    • Linear Equations

    A rectangle has perimeter $54$ cm.

    Its length is $5$ cm more than its width.

    What is the width of the rectangle?

    - -

    Solution

    Let $w$ be the width. Then the length is $w + 5$.

    Use the perimeter formula:

    $$ 2w + 2(w + 5) = 54 $$

    Expand and combine like terms:

    $$ 4w + 10 = 54 $$

    Subtract $10$:

    $$ 4w = 44 $$

    Divide by $4$:

    $$ w = 11 $$

    3.3Compare Ticket and Snack Prices

    Final | Problem 3.3 |
    • Systems of equations
    • Linear Equations

    At a concert, 2 tickets and 3 drinks cost \$34.

    Also, 1 ticket and 1 drink cost \$13.

    What is the price of one ticket?

    - -

    Solution

    Let $t$ be the ticket price and $d$ be the drink price.

    Set up the system:

    $$ \begin{aligned} 2t + 3d &= 34 \\ t + d &= 13 \end{aligned} $$

    Multiply the second equation by $2$:

    $$ 2t + 2d = 26 $$

    Subtract this from the first equation:

    $$ d = 8 $$

    Substitute back into $t + d = 13$:

    $$ t + 8 = 13 $$
    $$ t = 5 $$

    3.4Constant Rate Change

    Final | Problem 3.4 |
    • Linear Equations

    A water tank starts with $120$ liters.

    It gains $25$ liters per hour.

    After how many hours will it contain $245$ liters?

    - -

    Solution

    Let $h$ be the number of hours. Write the equation:

    $$ 120 + 25h = 245 $$

    Subtract $120$:

    $$ 25h = 125 $$

    Divide by $25$:

    $$ h = 5 $$

    3.5Proportional Reasoning in a Recipe

    Final | Problem 3.5 |
    • Fractions

    A recipe uses $3$ cups of flour for $8$ servings.

    If the same recipe is scaled to $16$ servings, how many cups of flour are needed?

    - -

    Solution

    Since $16$ is twice $8$, the flour amount also doubles.

    $$ 3 \times 2 = 6 $$

    So the recipe needs $6$ cups of flour.

    4.2Perpendicular Slope from Standard Form

    Final | Problem 4.2 |
    • Linear Equations

    The line

    $$ 2x + y = 11 $$

    has slope $-2$.

    What is the slope of a line perpendicular to it?

    - -

    Solution

    Rewrite the line in slope-intercept form:

    $$ y = -2x + 11 $$

    So the slope is $-2$.

    A perpendicular line has slope equal to the negative reciprocal:

    $$ m_\perp = \frac{1}{2} $$

    In decimal form, that is $0.5$.

    4.3Recover a Quadratic Constant from Its Roots

    • Zero-product property

    The quadratic

    $$ x^2 + bx + c $$

    has roots $4$ and $-3$.

    What is the value of $c$?

    - -

    Solution

    Write the quadratic using its roots:

    $$ (x - 4)(x + 3) $$

    Expand:

    $$ x^2 - x - 12 $$

    The constant term is

    $$ c = -12 $$

    4.4Cancel a Factor and Solve Carefully

    Final | Problem 4.4 |
    • Fractions
    • Distributing and factoring
    • Inverse Operations

    Solve for $x$, making sure to respect any restrictions:

    $$ \frac{x^2 - 25}{x - 5} = 12 $$
    - -

    Solution

    First note the restriction:

    $$ x \ne 5 $$

    Factor the numerator:

    $$ x^2 - 25 = (x - 5)(x + 5) $$

    So for $x \ne 5$,

    $$ \frac{(x - 5)(x + 5)}{x - 5} = x + 5 $$

    Now solve:

    $$ x + 5 = 12 $$
    $$ x = 7 $$
